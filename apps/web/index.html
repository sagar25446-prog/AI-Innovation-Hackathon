The loop

Understand → Plan → Teach → Check → Adapt

From your own material

Upload a PDF, DOCX, PPTX or paste notes. GuruFlow extracts page-numbered sections and cites real sources — it never invents a citation.

A timed lesson plan

Tell it how much time you have (5, 10, 20 or 60 minutes) and choose a study mode — Lesson, Exam prep, or Quick revision.

A talking visual teacher

An animated teacher with karaoke captions narrates each scene over a live circuit or graph. Switch language mid-lesson, tap voice on.

Checks understanding

Answer a question in any language. Get it wrong and GuruFlow diagnoses the exact misconception and re-teaches it differently.

Remembers you

Long-term learning profile, review flashcards, a 7-day spaced-revision plan and a final report with strong and weak concepts.

Your choice of teacher style

Pick a patient, socratic or coach personality. The content stays the same — the tone adapts to how you like to be taught.

WHY GURUFLOW

Most AI tutors answer questions.
This one notices what you got wrong.

Marking an answer incorrect tells a learner nothing they did not already know. GuruFlow names the belief behind the mistake and teaches it again, a different way.

The difference

The lesson changes when you do

Answer the checkpoint wrongly and the lesson physically grows: a new scene is written into it that attacks your specific misconception with a different representation - the equation, an analogy, and a graph - then re-asks the question.

7 scenes → 8 scenes after one wrong answer
Trust

It shows its sources

Every scene cites the page it came from, and the Evidence drawer shows the exact passage. When your material does not cover something, it says so instead of inventing a citation.

Reach

Fifteen teaching languages

English, Hindi and Hinglish are written by hand; twelve more are localised on demand. Equations are never translated, so I = V/R is identical in all of them.

Reliability

The demo cannot fail

The built-in chapter runs with no API key, no network and no GPU. Every optional part - the language model, the photoreal teacher, the voice - degrades to something that still works.

Video

Drawn, not dreamed

Animations are rendered programmatically rather than sampled from a video model. A generative model cannot be trusted to draw a correct circuit, and a plausible-but-wrong diagram would undo the grounding everything else rests on.

Memory

It remembers you

Mastery, weak concepts and resolved misconceptions persist between lessons, and feed a spaced revision plan built around what you actually struggled with.
